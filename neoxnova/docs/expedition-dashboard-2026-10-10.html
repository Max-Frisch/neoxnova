Recommendation

1 · STAY on the Frigate (227) — do not switch hulls

It is both the highest-throughput build (8.8×10⁸ pts/s, 3.4× the next hull; lowest crystal intensity 0.25/pt) and uniquely resilient to the alien high-roll tail that actually kills fleets: at ~250 ships the hard-alien case costs the Frigate 8% (100% win) while every other hull loses 55–100% and several are wiped outright (0% win). The earlier “switch to BB/BC” idea is wrong — those get annihilated by the tail.

2 · The real problem is crystal, not the hull

Rebuilding 84.6k Frigates needs ~846 B crystal; the account holds ~119 B. Scrap the useless free stockpile for crystal via the black-market ship trader (50% loss) — 88 M LF + 58 M LC + 53 M HF are dead weight (standard weapons, no specific-tech bonus, and they get wiped). Reserve Battle Recyclers (219) for cargo.

3 · Interim: keep slots flying from the free stockpile, but burnable

While Frigates rebuild, let drain.mainOrder fall back to the free laser/ion hulls (207 / 215 / 213 / 206) so no slot idles — but expect them to be far more expendable to aliens than the Frigate. Prefer them over standard-weapon fodder (LF 204 / HF 205).

4 · Do NOT raise general research 109/110/111

They sit at 18/18/18 but the Frigate techtree floor is 16/16/17. Every general level raises the alien's single rolled W/S/A roll too. Put future research into the attacker-only weapon techs (Laser/Ion/Plasma) and Arsenal, never the mirrored generals.
